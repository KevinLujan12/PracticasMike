Privacy Policy & Data Protection Governance

This document defines ethical data handling, vulnerability mitigation mechanisms, and the cryptographic storage policy implemented across academic projects developed by Kevin Lujan and Mauricio Prieto.

HMAC-SHA256 Encryption OWASP Top 10 Aligned Sovereign Client Storage

1. Privacy Principles & Scope of Application

Commitment to strict data minimization

Our applications (PotatoHub — Gourmet Selection and Hollow Earth Theory Research) operate under the rigorous principle of "Privacy by Design and by Default". We do not collect, transmit, or monetize personal identifiable information with any external server or third-party ad brokers.

All active session details, simulated operational credentials, and visual preferences (such as dark mode) reside exclusively within your client web browser (LocalStorage and SessionStorage).

2. Cryptographic Authentication & Web Tokens (JWT)

Structure, digital signing, and anti-tampering integrity

HMAC-SHA256 Signature

Each emitted token carries a cryptographic signature computed via the native JavaScript Web Crypto API. Any attempt to modify payload attributes (e.g., privilege escalation to Administrator) breaks signature verification and revokes the session immediately.

Expiration & Inactivity Rotation

Tokens feature a bounded 2-hour lifespan (exp timestamp). Furthermore, the client inactivity monitor revokes authentication if the user remains idle for more than 15 continuous minutes.

3. Active OWASP Top 10 Threat Mitigations

Active defenses embedded into application runtime

1
Brute-Force & Credential Stuffing Prevention Continuous access attempt monitoring. A progressive enforced lockout triggers after 5 consecutive failed attempts, paired with an artificial anti-timing delay to thwart automated bot scrapers.
2
Clickjacking Shield Strict X-Frame-Options: DENY and frame-ancestors 'none' Content-Security-Policy directives accompanied by client-side frame-busting logic that prevents embedding into concealed iframes.
3
Input Sanitization (XSS & Injection Defense) All search fields, text filters, and form inputs are sanitized and escaped with secure HTML entities prior to processing or rendering.
4
Hardened Production HTTP Headers (Vercel Edge) Full deployment configuration including Content-Security-Policy, X-Content-Type-Options: nosniff, Referrer-Policy: strict-origin-when-cross-origin, and Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS).

4. Sovereign Data Control & Immediate Purge

Right to absolute deletion of local sessions and caches

You maintain sovereign control over your device. Clicking the purge button irreversibly wipes all JWT tokens, audit logs, security locks, and interface preferences stored in this browser, redirecting you to a completely fresh session.
